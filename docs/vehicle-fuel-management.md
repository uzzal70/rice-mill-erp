# Vehicle & Fuel Management (Fleet Desk)

## Status
Complete

## 1. Business workflow
- **Purpose and actors:**
  - Track internal and contracted transport vehicles, fleet trips, mileage, driver allocations, fuel consumption logs, periodic servicing, breakdown maintenance, and regulatory document expiration compliance (Fitness, Tax Token, Insurance).
  - Primary actors: Fleet Manager, Transport Officer, Logistics Dispatcher, Factory Gate Officer, Accountant.
- **Preconditions and source documents:**
  - Registered vehicle master records (or plant equipment/generators for stationary fuel logs).
  - Source documents: Trip Sheets, Driver Logs, Filling Station Fuel Vouchers/Slips, Workshop Repair Bills, Transport Challans.
- **States and transitions:**
  - Trip: `PLANNED` -> `IN_TRANSIT` -> `COMPLETED`.
  - Vehicle: `ACTIVE` / `INACTIVE` / `UNDER_MAINTENANCE`.
  - Compliance: `VALID` -> `DUE_SOON` (within 30 days) -> `EXPIRED`.
- **Approval, posting, cancellation, correction/reversal:**
  - Trips and fuel logs are recorded against the company scope with immutable odometer readings.
  - Odometer inconsistencies (end odometer lower than start odometer) are rejected at the service layer.
- **Company/factory/location and ownership scope:**
  - Scoped by `companyId` and optionally tagged with `factoryId` for localized logistics cost centers.

## 3. Relationships
- `VehicleTrip` references `Company(id)` and `Vehicle(companyId, id)`.
- `FuelLog` references `Company(id)` and optionally `Vehicle(companyId, id)`.
- `VehicleMaintenance` references `Vehicle(companyId, id)`.
- Cascade & restrict rules: Foreign keys restrict company deletion when trips or logs exist.

## 4. Calculations
- **Trip Distance:**
  $$\text{Distance KM} = \max(0, \text{End Odometer} - \text{Start Odometer})$$
- **Total Transport Cost:**
  $$\text{Total Transport Cost} = \text{Fuel Cost} + \text{Toll / Other Expenses}$$
- **Fuel Cost per KM:**
  $$\text{Fuel Cost / KM} = \begin{cases} \frac{\text{Fuel Cost}}{\text{Distance KM}} & \text{if } \text{Distance KM} > 0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
- **Transport Cost per KM:**
  $$\text{Transport Cost / KM} = \begin{cases} \frac{\text{Total Transport Cost}}{\text{Distance KM}} & \text{if } \text{Distance KM} > 0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
- **Fuel Purchase Cost:**
  $$\text{Total Fuel Cost} = \text{Quantity (Liters)} \times \text{Rate Per Liter}$$
- **Rounding and precision:**
  - Distance: Decimal(18, 2)
  - Currency amounts: Decimal(18, 2)
  - Unit rates per KM: Decimal(18, 4)

## 5. Accounting impact
- Fuel expenses and workshop repairs feed into General Ledger operational and logistics expense accounts (e.g., Account 6030: Fuel & Lubricants, Account 6040: Vehicle Maintenance) under `ExpensesModule` or automatic accounting journals.
- Freight amounts generated by internal fleet reflect revenue or internal logistics recovery credit.

## 6. Inventory impact
- Not directly mutating grain inventory. Trip cargo details reference finished goods or paddy lots transported between godowns and delivery destinations. Fuel logs track fuel inventory consumption when drawing from central diesel storage tanks.

## 7. API
- `POST /fleet/vehicles?companyId=...` - Register vehicle in fleet.
- `GET /fleet/vehicles?companyId=...` - List vehicles with recent trips and logs.
- `POST /fleet/trips?companyId=...` - Record trip with automatic KM and Cost/KM calculations.
- `GET /fleet/trips?companyId=...&vehicleId=...` - List trip history.
- `POST /fleet/fuel-logs?companyId=...` - Record fuel purchase or dispense.
- `GET /fleet/fuel-logs?companyId=...&vehicleId=...` - List fuel logs.
- `POST /fleet/maintenance?companyId=...` - Record workshop servicing or repair.
- `GET /fleet/maintenance?companyId=...&vehicleId=...` - List maintenance history.
- `GET /fleet/analytics?companyId=...` - Real-time fleet KPI metrics and compliance alerts.

## 8. Permissions
- Requires `inventory:read` for viewing fleet logs and analytics.
- Requires `inventory:create` for creating vehicles, logging trips, fuel purchases, and maintenance.

## 9. Validation
- End odometer must be greater than or equal to start odometer (`endOdometer >= startOdometer`).
- Fuel quantity and rate per liter must be positive numbers.
- Unique trip numbers per company.

## 10. UI
